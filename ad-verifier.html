🛡️ OOH Verify · AI-Powered Proof of Posting

Don't just be seen — be verified.

Independent proof that your out-of-home campaign is really running — the right creative, at the right site, at the right time, in the right condition. Works across billboards, LED / digital boards, lamp-post banners, transit, and small-format ads, with geo-tagged, timestamped photo evidence.

1

Right creative

AI compares the on-site photo to the approved artwork — flagging the wrong ad, an expired one, damage, or obstruction.

2

Right site

The photo's GPS matches the booked site's coordinates — or, for transit wraps, the vehicle is identified.

3

Right time

The capture falls within the campaign's flight dates.

4

Right condition

The face is undamaged, unobstructed, and properly lit.

Capture proof of posting

Field demo — captures a live photo, your device GPS, and a timestamp, then checks it against the booked site. Works best on a phone at the site.

What this does today: real GPS + timestamp capture, a real distance check against the booked site's coordinates, a flight-window check, and AI creative verification — a vision model compares the on-site photo to the approved artwork and flags the wrong/expired creative, damage, obstruction, or poor lighting.
The full build adds: tamper-evident server timestamps, stored records with a live compliance dashboard, shareable proof-of-posting certificates, and — for DOOH — automated playout-log verification. This mirrors how global verifiers (OOH Trace, Seedooh, and AI field-audit platforms) work.
