📸 OOH Field Capture

Take the on-site photo (and video for LED). Your location & time are recorded automatically. Send to your operator when you have signal — no login needed.

💡 For LED/digital: you don't need to record the whole loop. Watch for your ad, then record just ~10–15 seconds while it's on the screen — or take a photo of it. Long clips are slow to compress and may be too large to send.

✂️ Trim clip

Set the start and end to just the part showing your ad, then Trim & save. Location & time are kept.

👁 Review before sending

Check this is the right shot. Only this one is sent to the operator.
