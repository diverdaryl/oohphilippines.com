Billboard Directory Philippines: Find & Compare Sites in One Place

Published 2026  ·  OOH Philippines  ·  7 min read

If you've ever tried to find billboard advertising options in the Philippines, you already know the problem: pricing and availability are scattered across dozens of operators, none of whom list their inventory in the same place. There was no single site where you could search billboards by location, compare formats, and see what's actually available — until now.

OOHPhilippines.com is the Philippines' first dedicated billboard directory, built to solve exactly that problem. This guide walks through what the directory covers, how it works, and how advertisers, agencies, and operators each use it.

What Is a Billboard Directory, and Why Does the Philippines Need One?

A billboard directory is a searchable database of out-of-home (OOH) advertising sites — their locations, specifications, and (where available) pricing and availability — aggregated from multiple operators into one place. Instead of contacting each billboard company individually to ask "what do you have on EDSA?" or "what's available in Cebu?", you search once and see the options across operators side by side.

The Philippine OOH market has historically lacked this. Billboard companies in the Philippines range from large national operators to small regional players, and most only list inventory through direct sales contact — meaning advertisers had to know who to call, and agencies had to maintain their own private spreadsheets of contacts and rate cards. A public, searchable directory closes that gap.

What's Inside the OOHPhilippines.com Directory

The directory currently holds 2,000+ billboard listings sourced from operators across the country, covering both Metro Manila and key provincial markets:

Metro Manila Cebu Davao Bacolod Iloilo Cagayan de Oro Baguio & Provincial Luzon

Within Metro Manila, coverage spans the major high-traffic corridors advertisers care about most — EDSA, C5, Commonwealth Avenue, Quezon Avenue, NLEX, and SLEX — along with secondary roads across Caloocan, Marikina, Antipolo, and beyond. Each listing includes:

  • Exact location — corridor, city, and facing direction
  • Format — static, LED/digital, or lamp post banner
  • Size and specs — structure dimensions and illumination
  • Operator — the actual company that owns or manages the site
  • Availability status and, for subscribers, audience reach estimates based on traffic data for that corridor

How Advertisers and Agencies Use It

Instead of cold-calling operators one by one, you can search by city, corridor, or billboard type and get a shortlist in minutes. This is especially useful when:

Planning a Multi-City Campaign

National brands running campaigns across Metro Manila, Cebu, and Davao simultaneously can compare inventory across all three markets without juggling separate operator relationships for each city.

Scouting Before Committing Budget

Media planners can pull together a realistic shortlist and rough budget range before reaching out to operators — useful for early-stage client proposals where exact quotes aren't needed yet.

Finding Regional Options Fast

Provincial markets like Iloilo, Bacolod, or CDO are underserved by most media buying agencies simply because there's no efficient way to see what's there. A directory makes regional expansion practical.

Note: This directory lists operators and their inventory — it is not a media buying agency and does not act as a reseller. You connect and transact directly with the operator who owns each site.

How Billboard Operators Get Listed

Operators list their inventory for free — the directory is built from real operator-submitted data, not scraped or estimated listings. Operators who want more visibility can opt into Featured (₱1,500/mo) or Sponsored (₱3,000/mo) placements, which surface their sites higher in relevant searches and, for Featured listings, show the full audience-reach breakdown to every visitor rather than just subscribers.

This matters for smaller and regional operators in particular — companies that don't have the marketing budget of the largest national players finally have a channel to be discovered by advertisers who wouldn't have found them otherwise.

Billboard Directory vs. Other Ways to Book OOH

Going Direct to Operators

  • Requires knowing which operator has inventory where
  • Separate negotiation and contact for every company
  • No way to compare pricing or availability side by side

Using a Billboard Directory

  • See inventory across operators in one search
  • Compare location, format, and specs before reaching out
  • Still connect and negotiate directly with the operator — no markup

A media buying agency is a third option — useful if you want someone to handle placement and negotiation for you, but it typically comes with a commission on top of the operator's rate. A directory sits in between: more efficient than going fully direct, without adding an intermediary fee.
