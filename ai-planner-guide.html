The AI reads your brief, ranks the best-fit billboards from the database, and writes a persuasive, client-ready pitch grounded in each site's real reach data.

Step by step

1. Fill the brief

Brand, category, target area, objective, and the Monthly rate you're quoting. The rate drives the ROI note — leave it blank and ROI stays empty.

2. Get ranked sites (free)

The AI scores every site on location, reach, and audience match. Type a refinement ("near schools", "billboards only") to re-rank.

3. Pick a site — by code OR name

Type a site code (e.g. F1236) or its display name (e.g. "SM City Bacolod") to preview it free, then generate the full pitch.
4. Build a campaign (2 ways)

Cart: tap "➕ Add to campaign" on ranked sites — the cart persists across areas. Or use "🧾 Enter site codes" and paste the codes directly.

5. Generate the combined pitch

One proposal across all chosen sites, with per-site reasons and an executive summary.

6. Download & send

Export as a PDF or an editable PowerPoint (replaceable photo, clickable map pin) — ready to email the client.

What's data vs. what's AI — say this to clients

✔ Grounded in data

  • Reach / impressions from real traffic counts
  • Site location, size, format, nearby places
  • ROI note uses the rate YOU entered

🤖 Written by AI

  • The persuasive copy & positioning angle
  • Best-fit advertiser categories per site
  • It never invents reach figures — every number traces to traffic data

Do

  • Always enter the Monthly rate so the client sees ROI & CPM.
  • Preview free first, then spend a credit only when the client is interested.
  • Use the editable PowerPoint to rebrand or drop into your own template.
  • Lead with the site's reach data and the "1 in N" break-even logic.

Don't

  • Don't present the AI pitch as a guarantee of results.
  • Don't edit reach numbers by hand — keep them traceable to data.
  • Don't burn credits generating full pitches before a client bites.
  • Don't forget to swap in the client's real, updated site photo.

"Tell me the brand and where you want to advertise, and I'll pull up the best-fit billboards ranked by real traffic and audience match. We can preview any of them free — and when you like one, I'll generate a full proposal you can take to your client, as a PDF or an editable PowerPoint. Every reach number comes from traffic data, not guesswork."
